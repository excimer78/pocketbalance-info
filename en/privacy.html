This policy describes how PocketBalance handles data and how support messages sent to the developer are handled.

Developer and contact

PocketBalance is developed by Oliver Löser. The controller of personal data in support messages received by the developer is Oliver Löser. Contact and privacy requests: pocketbalance.tuki@outlook.com.

Data processed on your device

The app processes the accounts, transactions, budgets, loans, investments, goals, names, notes, receipt images and recognised receipt text that you enter to help you track your finances. Receipt text recognition and financial calculations run on your device. Settings and remembered receipt recipients are stored locally. Camera access is requested for capturing a receipt. App lock uses device authentication; the app does not receive Face ID or Touch ID biometric data.

Optional iCloud

iCloud storage is optional. When you enable it, financial data and receipts are transferred to your Apple Account’s private CloudKit database in Apple’s service. Turning off iCloud storage in PocketBalance does not delete data already stored in iCloud. Changes and deletions may synchronise. Use one primary iPhone at a time to edit your finances. Device backups are controlled separately by your device and Apple Account settings.

Apple Watch

If you use the watch app, your iPhone and paired Apple Watch exchange financial summaries, available choices and expenses entered on the watch using WatchConnectivity. Data is also cached on the watch for complications. An offline watch or complication may show the last received data. Hiding amounts does not delete the data.

Encrypted backups

You can export your financial data and receipts to an encrypted .talousbackup file. You choose where to save it and protect it with a passphrase or PIN. Depending on your choice, the file may be stored locally or in a cloud service. The app does not send the backup to the developer. Keep the passphrase or PIN required for recovery safe. A reminder in the app does not create a backup automatically.

Retention and deletion

Financial data remains on your device until you delete it or delete the app. Recovery and transfer operations may leave earlier local database copies in the app’s storage. Deleting the app entirely from your iPhone removes its local app storage; removing its icon from the Home Screen is not enough. Deleting the app does not delete iCloud data or backups that you saved yourself. Manage iCloud data in your Apple Account’s iCloud settings, and delete backup files separately from the places where you saved them. Manage data remaining on the watch separately by deleting the watch app when needed.

Tracking and diagnostics

The app has no advertising, advertising tracking or separate developer analytics service. Financial data and receipt images are not sent to a developer server or an external AI service. Apple may provide the developer with crash and usage information according to your device’s diagnostics settings and the distribution method. Reporting during TestFlight testing differs from App Store distribution. Apple’s services are subject to Apple’s privacy practices.

Email support data

If you contact pocketbalance.tuki@outlook.com, the developer receives your sender address, any name you provide, and the message and attachments you choose to send. The data is used to respond to your enquiry and resolve the support issue. Processing is based on the developer’s legitimate interest in handling a user-initiated enquiry. The data is not used for marketing. Support messages pass through email services, including Microsoft Outlook.com; those services may involve transfers outside the EEA under their privacy practices. Messages are kept for the time needed to handle the support issue and any related claims. Data that is no longer needed is deleted. Do not send passwords, real financial data, receipt images or backups.

Privacy requests and rights

You can send a request about personal data processed in support messages to pocketbalance.tuki@outlook.com. Depending on the circumstances and legal conditions, you may have rights to access, correct or delete the data, restrict processing and object to processing based on legitimate interests. You may lodge a complaint with the competent data protection authority; in Finland, the Office of the Data Protection Ombudsman (tietosuoja.fi). The developer cannot retrieve or delete the financial data stored on your device or in your Apple Account’s private iCloud on your behalf.

Privacy on these web pages

The pages themselves have no analytics, advertising, cookies or forms. They do not read app or iCloud data. If the pages are served through GitHub Pages, GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes.
