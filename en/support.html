Before contacting support

Describe what you tried to do and what happened. You may include the version and build number shown on the app’s About and support page and your device’s operating system version. Use a fictional example if needed.

Do not send passwords, PINs, Apple Account credentials, real financial data, receipt images or .talousbackup files. Hide personal details and amounts in screenshots as well.
